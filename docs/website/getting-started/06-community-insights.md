# Community Insights

Community Insights is your at-a-glance view of how the community is doing, computed from your own data and shown right inside wp-admin. It answers the questions every owner asks - how many members do I have, are they active, is anyone posting - without exporting anything or connecting a third-party analytics tool. You find it under **BuddyNext > Engagement > Insights**, and a compact version also appears on your main WordPress dashboard.

![The BuddyNext Insights screen with member, post, space and engagement metrics and a new-members chart](../images/admin-insights.webp)

## Why use it

Running a community is easier when you can see whether it is growing or stalling. A single number - "12 members" - tells you little; "12 members, up 4 this week, 8 active in the last 30 days" tells you the community is small but alive. Insights gives you that context in a few cards so you can spot momentum, notice a quiet week, and decide when to nudge things along with an announcement or a new space. It is included free, so every owner has the basics from day one.

## The metrics

The **At a glance** panel shows the headline numbers, several with a short-term trend:

| Metric | What it tells you |
|--------|-------------------|
| Members | Total members, with how many joined this week. |
| Active (30 days) | How many members did something in the last month, shown as a share of your total. |
| Posts | Total posts, with how many were published this week. |
| Spaces | How many spaces exist in the community. |
| Comments | Total comments across the community. |
| Reactions | Total reactions members have given. |
| Connections | Total accepted connections between members. |
| Follows | Total follow relationships. |

Below the cards, a **New members** chart plots signups across the last 14 days, so you can see growth as a shape rather than a single figure.

## The dashboard widget

BuddyNext also adds a small **BuddyNext community** widget to the standard WordPress dashboard - the first screen you see when you log in. It lists your key totals (members, active members, posts, spaces) with the same weekly trends, plus a "View all insights" link that takes you to the full screen. If you only ever glance at the dashboard, the pulse of your community is still right there.

## Free Insights vs Pro Analytics

Community Insights covers the essentials every owner needs and is part of the free plugin. If you want deeper reporting - trends over longer periods, breakdowns, and more detailed engagement views - that lives in BuddyNext Pro Analytics. Insights is the quick daily read; Analytics is the full report. See the Pro documentation for what Analytics adds.

> **Note:** Every number here is calculated from your own community data on your own site. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is nothing to set up - the figures appear as soon as members start using the community.
