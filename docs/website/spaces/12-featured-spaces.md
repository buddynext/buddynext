# Feature Spaces for New Members

You can choose which spaces to highlight so new members start where you want them, instead of a near-random list. Featured spaces show up first in the Spaces directory and in the new-member welcome flow.

## Why use it

On a new community there are no member counts or activity yet, so every automatic "popular" or "suggested" list is close to random, exactly when a newcomer most needs steering. Featuring a handful of spaces fixes that:

- **You decide the starting point.** The spaces you feature lead the directory and onboarding, so a new member's first click is one you chose.
- **It follows them across the site.** Featured spaces lead the directory sidebar on desktop, a strip at the top on mobile, the onboarding "Spaces" step, and get a nudge in the suggestions members see in the feed.
- **No setup trap.** If you have not featured anything but you auto-join new members to some spaces, those become the featured set automatically, so the section is never empty for no reason.

## How it works (site owner, in the admin)

Everything is under **BuddyNext > Spaces > Directory**.

### Featuring a space

- From the **"…" menu** on any space row, choose **Feature** (or **Unfeature**). Featured spaces show a **Featured** badge, and a **Featured (n)** filter shows just them.
- You can feature **up to 6** spaces. Trying to feature a 7th tells you to unfeature one first.
- Select several rows and use the **bulk action** to feature or unfeature them together.

### Ordering them

The **Featured spaces** section at the top of the Directory shows your featured spaces in order. Drag to reorder, or use the up/down buttons, and remove one with the ×. Type a name in **Add a space** to search and add another. Changes save as you make them.

The order matters: it is the order members see, top to bottom.

## What members see

- **Desktop / tablet:** a **Featured** card at the top of the Spaces directory sidebar.
- **Phone:** a horizontal **Featured spaces** strip above the directory list (the sidebar is hidden on small screens).
- **New members:** your featured spaces lead the "Spaces" step of the welcome flow (open spaces only there, since that step is one-click join).
- **In the feed:** featured spaces a member has not joined get a gentle boost in their suggested spaces, behind their strongest personal matches.

## Good to know

- **Private and secret spaces stay private.** You can feature them, but they are only shown to people who can already see them. A featured secret space never appears to someone who should not know it exists.
- **Archived or deleted spaces drop out** of the featured lists automatically.
- **It is your data, not a setting.** "Restore defaults" on the Spaces settings never clears your featured list.
- **Featured spaces are not pinned into the main directory grid** or its sorts; they lead the discovery surfaces above, and the grid stays as-is.
