# Recipe: Make It Fully Your Own

**Goal:** a community that looks and feels like your brand, with no sign it runs on BuddyNext.
**You'll need:** BuddyNext Free for branding; Pro for full white-label and per-space branding.
**Time:** about 30 minutes.

## Before you start

- Have your logo, brand colours, and (for Pro white-label) the plugin name you want members and admins to see.
- Decide how far to take it: brand the front end only, or rename the whole thing in wp-admin too.

## Steps

1. **Set your colours, logo, and appearance.** Match the community to your site so it reads as one product, in light and dark mode.
   Full guide: [Appearance and Branding](../getting-started/07-appearance-and-branding.md).

2. **White-label the plugin (Pro).** Rename the admin menu label so the plugin presents under your own name rather than "BuddyNext".
   Full guide: [White-label](../pro/20-white-label.md).

3. **Brand individual spaces (Pro).** Give flagship spaces their own header and identity, so a client or premium space feels bespoke.
   Full guide: [Space Branding](../pro/21-space-branding.md).

4. **Customize expression (Pro).** Replace the default reaction set with reactions that fit your community's voice.
   Full guide: [Custom Reactions](../pro/07-custom-reactions.md).

5. **Restyle deeper with a child theme (developers).** For pixel-level control, override templates and tokens from your theme rather than editing the plugin.
   Full guide: [Child Theme Template Overrides](../developer-guide/49-child-theme-template-overrides.md) and [Customizing Cards](../developer-guide/48-customizing-cards.md).

## What your members see

Members see your brand, not ours - your colours, your logo, your reactions, and (with white-label) your name throughout. The community feels native to your site rather than like a bolted-on plugin.
