# Recipe: Run a Private or Invite-Only Space

**Goal:** a space only the right people can see or join - a client project, a paid cohort, a staff-only room.
**You'll need:** BuddyNext Free for a private space; add Pro if you want to charge for access.
**Time:** about 15 minutes.

## Before you start

Decide how tight the door needs to be:

- **Private** - anyone can see the space exists and ask to join; you approve requests.
- **Secret** - the space is invisible to non-members; people get in only when you add them.
- **Paid** - entry requires an active subscription (needs Pro).

## Steps

1. **Create the space and set its type.** Choose Private or Secret when you create it, so it is closed before anyone can wander in.
   Full guide: [Creating a Space](../spaces/02-creating-a-space.md) and [Space Types and Privacy](../spaces/03-space-types-and-privacy.md).

2. **Add your people (or approve requests).** For a secret space, add members directly. For a private space, approve the join requests that come in.
   Full guide: [Managing Members](../spaces/04-managing-members.md).

3. **Appoint moderators.** For anything beyond a handful of people, give one or two trusted members moderator rights so you are not the only one keeping order.
   Full guide: [Roles and Moderators](../spaces/05-roles-and-moderators.md). To remove someone for good, see [Space Bans](../spaces/06-space-bans.md).

4. **(Optional) Charge for entry.** To make it a paid room, require a membership plan to join. Non-subscribers see a subscribe prompt instead of the feed.
   Full guide: [Gated Spaces](../pro/02-gated-spaces.md). To set up the plan and payments first, follow [Launch a Paid Community](01-launch-a-paid-community.md).

## What your members see

A private space shows a "Request to join" button until they are approved; a secret space does not appear in the directory or search at all until they are a member. Once in, it works exactly like any other space.
